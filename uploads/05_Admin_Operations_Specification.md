# DLT Admin & Operations Specification

## 1. Roles

### Super Admin
Full access, payment reconciliation, refunds/overrides, sensitive student information, manual boarding, audit logs.

### Operations Admin
Trips, bookings, passengers, boarding, operational vehicle/trip management, feedback and permitted student data.

### Boarding Staff
Assigned trip manifest, seat map and QR scanning. No financial/admin override.

## 2. Dashboard
Show today's trips, passengers, seats, revenue, boarding progress, alerts and quick actions.

## 3. Trip Management
Create Draft, validate, publish/open, close booking, board, depart, complete or cancel.

## 4. Vehicle Management
Manage vehicle status, registration, capacity and seat configuration. Prevent invalid/overlapping assignments.

## 5. Booking Management
Overview + Passengers + Payment + Boarding + Activity/Audit tabs.

## 6. Payment Reconciliation
Super Admin only. Support successful, failed, pending, duplicate, discrepancy, refund pending, refunded and manual/external records.

## 7. Boarding
Live manifest, QR scanner, manual boarding, no-show and denied-boarding workflows.

## 8. Reports
Bookings, revenue, passengers, occupancy, manifests, boarding, no-shows, payments and refunds. CSV/Excel exports.

## 9. Audit
Important changes record actor, action, previous value, new value, timestamp, affected entity and reason.

## 10. Deletion
Important operational/financial/boarding records are not permanently deleted.
