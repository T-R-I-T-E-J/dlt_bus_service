# DLT UX/UI Design Specification

## 1. Design Direction
Premium, clean, modern, student-focused and cinematic.

## 2. Information Architecture
### Public
Home, Book, How It Works, Route, Why DLT, About, Help/FAQ, Contact, legal/policy pages.

### Student
Sign In, Sign Up, Dashboard, My Trips, Boarding Passes, Profile.

### Admin
Separate Admin interface.

## 3. Homepage
The road is the visual backbone. The bus continuously travels while scroll controls the journey.

### Woxsen
Starting hero and first major information card.

### Journey
Continuous road travel with cinematic camera movement.

### Miyapur
Destination hero. The bus stops and a destination card appears.

### Booking transition
The road continues and trip cards emerge from the 3D environment before transforming into normal interactive UI.

## 4. Booking
Hybrid layout:
- Main interactive area
- Persistent booking summary

## 5. Seat Selector
2D interactive 2+2 map with subtle 3D styling. Show available, selected, held, booked and reserved states.

## 6. Passenger Forms
Accordion/hybrid. Passenger 1 expanded by default; additional passengers collapsible.

## 7. Confirmation
Show compact passenger summary, Booking ID, Boarding Code and individual pass access.

## 8. Dashboard
Upcoming trip, boarding pass, quick actions and recent trips.

## 9. Accessibility
Keyboard support, labels, focus states, contrast, reduced motion and a non-3D core booking path.

## 10. TBD
Final typography, colors, exact animation choreography, mobile 3D treatment and final copy.
