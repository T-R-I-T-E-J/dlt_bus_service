# DLT Security, Privacy & Compliance Specification

## 1. Principles
- Least privilege
- Role-based access
- Server-side authorization
- Secure password storage
- Secure sessions
- Rate limiting
- CAPTCHA where appropriate
- Secure QR tokens
- No personal information in QR payload
- Server-side payment verification
- Audit sensitive actions

## 2. Personal Data
Student records may include name, Student ID, phone, email and emergency contact.

Access must be role-based.

## 3. Emergency Contact
Controlled operational access; not shown to Boarding Staff by default.

## 4. Financial Data
Payment references and reconciliation records are retained; card/payment credentials are not stored by DLT unless explicitly required and permitted by the payment architecture.

## 5. Deletion
Important operational, financial and audit records are retained. Personal data can be anonymized/deleted where legally possible after account deletion review.

## 6. Audit
Sensitive actions and Admin overrides are logged.

## 7. Legal
Final Terms, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and other legal wording require separate legal review.

Exact periods are TBD pending legal/accounting requirements.
