How it works

  1. Step 01

    Find your trip

    Pick a departure from the open dates. No account needed to look.

  2. Step 02

    Select your seat

    Window or aisle, up to five seats. Your choice is held for 10 minutes.

  3. Step 03

    Passenger details

    Name, student ID and phone for every passenger travelling.

  4. Step 04

    Pay securely

    One payment covers the whole booking, in INR. We confirm it on our side before issuing your seats.

  5. Step 05

    Board

    Show the QR on your pass at the door. Each passenger gets their own.

Why DLT

One route, done properly

A fixed departure, a named pickup point and a seat with your number on it. No haggling, no waiting at the gate.

Verified before you board

Every passenger carries a secure QR pass checked against the manifest at the door. Your seat cannot be sold twice.

Built for students

₹{{ fare }} flat, five passengers on one payment, and a full refund if you cancel more than 24 hours out.

About DLT

Getting to the city shouldn't be the hardest part of the week

DLT gives Woxsen students a predictable way to reach Miyapur: published departures, a named pickup point, a numbered seat and a fixed launch fare of ₹{{ fare }}.

We are starting with Woxsen to Miyapur and adding Miyapur to Woxsen when the schedule is ready. The booking system already supports both directions, so each new trip works the same way.
