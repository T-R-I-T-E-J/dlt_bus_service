How it works

  1. Step 01

    Find your trip

    Pick a departure from the open dates. No account needed to look.

  2. Step 02

    Select your seat

    Window or aisle, up to five seats. Your choice is held for 10 minutes.

  3. Step 03

    Passenger details

    Name, student ID and phone for every passenger travelling.

  4. Step 04

    Pay securely

    One payment covers the whole booking, in INR. We confirm it on our side before issuing your seats.

  5. Step 05

    Board

    Show the QR on your pass at the door. Each passenger gets their own.

Why DLT

One route, done properly

A fixed departure, a named pickup point and a seat with your number on it. No haggling, no waiting at the gate.

Verified before you board

Every passenger carries a secure QR pass checked against the manifest at the door. Your seat cannot be sold twice.

Built for students

₹{{ fare }} flat, five passengers on one payment, and a full refund if you cancel more than 12 hours out.

About DLT

Getting to the city shouldn't be the hardest part of the week

Woxsen sits an hour and a half outside Hyderabad. Students who need to get to the city have been left with whatever turns up: shared cabs settled by argument at the gate, buses that leave when they are full rather than when they are due, and no way to know in advance whether there will be a seat at all.

DLT is the opposite of that. One route, published departures, a named pickup point, a numbered seat and a fixed fare of ₹{{ fare }}. You know before you leave your room that you have a seat, which coach it is on, and when it goes.

We are starting with a single route on purpose. Woxsen to Miyapur, done properly, is worth more than five routes done approximately — and everything here is built so the second route changes nothing about how the first one works.
